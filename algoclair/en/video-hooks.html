5 video hooks to hold attention in the first 3 seconds

The first seconds decide the rest. Here are 5 hook templates to adapt to your topic.

Updated October 24, 2026

Every new video is first tested on a small audience. If people swipe it away in the first seconds, distribution stops. The hook is the most important moment.

3 rules

  1. Start with the strongest moment, no "Hi, today…".
  2. Show and say the same thing: many people watch with the sound off.
  3. Keep the promise, or people leave.

5 hooks to copy

Nobody talks about [detail], yet it changes everything.

I tried [thing] for [time]. Here's what happened.

3 [tools / places / settings] I wish I'd known sooner.

Send this to the person who [recognizable habit].

Before / after: [transformation] in [time].
