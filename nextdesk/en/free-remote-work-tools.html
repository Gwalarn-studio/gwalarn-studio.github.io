The Best Free Tools for Remote Work

One reliable free tool for each remote work need, what the free plan really allows, and how to avoid installing too many.

Updated November 21, 2026
In short
  • Remote work needs six types of tools: video, chat, shared documents, tasks, notes and passwords.
  • Almost all exist in a free version that is enough for a freelancer, a small team or a job seeker.
  • Free plans change often: check the limits on the vendor's site before you commit.
  • At a company, use the tools your employer provides, especially for sensitive data.

Remote work runs on a few well-chosen tools. You don't need expensive subscriptions to start: most remote work software has a free plan, often enough for one person or a small team. The real risk is elsewhere: installing ten, scattering your attention and losing information between apps. Here is a short selection, one tool per need, with what to know about each free plan.

The six needs of remote work

NeedWhat it's for
VideoMeetings, interviews, team check-ins.
ChatQuick exchanges, questions, coordination.
Shared documentsWriting, spreadsheets and slides together.
Tasks and projectsWho does what, by when.
Notes and docsKeeping information in one place.
Passwords and securityProtecting accounts and data.

Start with these six. Add a tool only when a real problem shows up.
