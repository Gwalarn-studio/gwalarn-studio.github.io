Career Change to a Remote Job: A 90-Day Plan

A three-month plan to move from an on-site job to a remote one: choose, learn, prove, then apply.

Updated November 21, 2026
In short
  • A successful career change starts from what you already know how to do, not from a blank page.
  • Pick a job where remote work is common, and check that it is really hiring.
  • A course is not enough: you need 2 or 3 concrete projects to show.
  • Test the job for free before paying for training.

You work in a job where remote work is impossible (retail, logistics, care, hospitality, manufacturing) and you'd like to work from home? It is a realistic goal, if you go about it methodically. Career changes that fail often start with a course picked at random. Those that succeed start from existing skills, test the job before changing everything, and build proof. Here is a 90-day plan to adapt to your pace.

Jobs where remote work is common

Remote work is most common in jobs done on a computer, where the result is delivered as files or remote exchanges.
